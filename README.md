 📚 Smart Study Planner

A modern and intelligent study planning application designed to help students organize their study schedules, manage time effectively, and achieve their academic goals. Smart Study Planner provides personalized study recommendations, visual progress tracking, and balanced time distribution to make learning more productive and stress-free.

🚀 Features:

 📊 Dashboard Overview:
 
. Clean and interactive dashboard
. Displays daily study progress
. Shows upcoming study tasks
. Quick access to study plans
. Progress tracking with visual charts
. Weekly and monthly study summaries

💡 Smart Study Tips:

. Personalized study recommendations
. Focus on high-priority subjects
.  Pomodoro technique suggestions
. Break reminders to avoid burnout
. Daily motivation and productivity tips
.  Healthy study habits guidance

 ⏰ Study Time Distribution:
 
The planner intelligently distributes study time based on:

. Subject priority
. Exam schedules
. Difficulty level of subjects
. Available study hours
. Daily learning goals
. Balanced workload across the week
. This helps students maintain consistency without feeling overwhelmed.

 🎯 Why is it Useful?

Smart Study Planner helps students develop better study habits by creating structured and balanced study schedules. Instead of spending time deciding what to study next, students can focus on learning while the planner manages their timetable.

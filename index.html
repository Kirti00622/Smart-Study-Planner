About

Why Smart Planner?

Smart Study Planner AI was built by educators and engineers who understand the challenges students face during exam preparation. Our platform leverages artificial intelligence to create personalized study schedules that adapt to your unique learning style.

We believe that every student deserves a tailored study experience. By analyzing your subject strengths, exam dates, and available study time, our AI creates optimal timetables that maximize retention and minimize stress.
